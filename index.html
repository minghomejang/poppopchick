隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日　開發者：Pop Pop Chick 團隊

「啵啵雞 Pop Pop Chick」（以下稱「本遊戲」）重視你的隱私。本遊戲不需要註冊帳號，我們不會要求你的姓名、電子郵件或電話。

1. 儲存在你裝置上的資料

  • 遊戲進度、星數、設定（語言、音效、主題）只儲存在你的裝置上，不會上傳到我們的伺服器。
  • 刪除本遊戲即會刪除這些資料。

2. 購買（App 內購買）

  • 付款由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得你的信用卡或付款資訊。
  • 我們使用 RevenueCat 驗證購買與「恢復購買」，RevenueCat 會處理匿名的 App 使用者識別碼與購買紀錄，僅用於提供你已購買的內容。

3. 廣告

  • 免費版本會顯示 Google AdMob 廣告（只在過關後按「下一關」時，以及你自己選擇觀看的獎勵廣告）。
  • 若你在 Apple 的「允許追蹤」詢問中選擇允許，AdMob 可能使用廣告識別碼（IDFA）提供更相關的廣告；選擇不允許時只會顯示非個人化廣告。你可以隨時在 iPhone「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」更改。
  • AdMob 可能處理裝置資訊、大略位置（依 IP 位址）及廣告互動資料，詳見 Google 廣告政策。
  • 購買「去除廣告」或「豪華包」後不會再顯示插頁廣告。

4. Game Center

若你使用排行榜，你的 Game Center 暱稱與分數會由 Apple 處理並顯示給你的好友或其他玩家。

5. 我們不做的事

  • 不販售你的個人資料。
  • 不收集通訊錄、照片、麥克風、相機或精確位置。

6. 兒童

本遊戲適合所有年齡，但不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

7. 政策變更與聯絡

政策若有變更會更新於本頁並修改生效日期。任何問題請來信：bennytsengstudio@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 6, 2026　Developer: the Pop Pop Chick team

Pop Pop Chick (“the Game”) respects your privacy. The Game does not require an account and never asks for your name, email or phone number.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Game progress, stars and settings (language, sound, theme) are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Deleting the Game deletes this data.

2. In-app purchases

  • Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user ID and purchase history solely to deliver what you bought.

3. Advertising

  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads (only after you tap “Next level” following a cleared level, and rewarded ads you choose to watch).
  • If you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, AdMob may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads; otherwise only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • AdMob may process device information, approximate location (from IP address) and ad interaction data. See Google’s advertising policies.
  • After purchasing “Remove Ads” or the “Deluxe Pack”, interstitial ads are no longer shown.

4. Game Center

If you use leaderboards, your Game Center nickname and scores are processed by Apple and shown to your friends or other players.

5. What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

6. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

7. Changes and contact

Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: bennytsengstudio@gmail.com

支援 Support

購買的東西不見了？ Missing purchases?

打開遊戲 → 商店（或設定）→「恢復購買」。請確認使用的是當初購買的 Apple 帳號。
Open the Game → Shop (or Settings) → “Restore Purchases”, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

卡關了？ Stuck on a level?

按 💡 提示可看接下來 3 步；按「上一步」或「重來」也不會扣任何東西。
Tap 💡 Hint to see the next 3 moves. Undo and Restart are always free.

遊戲進度 Progress

進度儲存在裝置上；刪除 App 會清除進度。
Progress is saved on your device; deleting the app erases it.
